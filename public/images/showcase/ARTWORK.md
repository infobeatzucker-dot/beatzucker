## Prompt set

Shared prompt: Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: square cover artwork for an audio comparison carousel on the premium dark Beatzucker mastering website. Generate ONE finished square image, edge-to-edge, no UI, no borders, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark. Primary subject: [scene below]. Style: cohesive high-end cinematic music cover, tactile atmospheric photography mixed with polished visual art, restrained grain, richly detailed but readable at thumbnail size, controlled neon rather than oversaturated. Composition: strong central scene, dark lower quarter suitable for white HTML title overlay; middle lower centre may be covered by a play button so avoid crucial detail there. Palette: midnight navy, violet, cyan with scene-specific warm accents. Original artwork, no real musician likenesses.

- on-tour: Cinematic night road towards distant stage lights, luminous magenta and cyan reflections on wet asphalt, a subtle unbranded touring vehicle silhouette far away, a feeling of motion and anticipation. No readable road signs.
